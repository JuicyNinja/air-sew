How it works

Designed with you, start to finish.

Five steps, one person who runs the shop, and a portal that shows you every stage. No guessing, no going dark.

01Start your design02Design session03Drop-off04Build05Delivery
01

Start your design

About ten minutes online

Enter your tail number and we pull your aircraft from the FAA registry. Answer ten questions about how you fly and who flies with you, add a photo, and see a concept of your new cabin. We call you within one business day.

02

Design session

In person or by video

Feel the leathers, pick colors and stitching, and settle the details. You leave with a written quote and your completion date.

03

Drop-off

Fly in, or we arrange the ferry

We walk the airplane with you, photograph every surface, and open your client portal.

04

Build

Watch it in your portal

Every stage is photographed and signed off. Anything unexpected comes to you with photos and a price before we touch it.

05

Delivery

Signed off, paperwork in hand

We walk the cabin with you, make the logbook entries, and hand over burn certs and your records packet.

Downtime

Your date, in writing

No two cabins are the same, so we don't publish typical timelines. We give you a real date for your airplane instead.

01

Set after your design session

Once scope and materials are chosen, we set your completion date.

02

Written into your quote

The date is part of what you approve, not a verbal estimate.

03

Tracked in your portal

You watch it hold, stage by stage. If a finding could move it, you hear first, with photos and options.
