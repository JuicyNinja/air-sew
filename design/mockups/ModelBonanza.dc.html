What wears out on a Bonanza

After [number] Bonanzas through our hangar, we know where they tire first, and how to make the fix last longer than the original.

Sun-baked seat leather

Big side windows cook the seat tops. We rebuild the foam and re-cover in UV-stable, burn-certified leather.

Cracked sidewalls and window trim

Original plastics go brittle. We repair, refinish or 3D-print new trim, then wrap panels to match your seats.

Tired aft doors and club seats

The aft double doors and club seats take the most traffic. We reinforce wear points and rebuild seat mechanisms.
