What it means for you

Perfect symmetry

Left and right seats, port and starboard panels: identical, stitch for stitch.

Spares that fit

Your patterns stay on file. Scuff a panel in five years and we cut a new one that drops right in.

Less waste, lower cost

Pieces are nested on the hide by software, so you pay for less leather.

Process

From your airplane to the machine and back

  1. 01
    Pattern
    We pattern your seats and panels once, by hand, right on your airplane.
  2. 02
    Digitize
    The patterns become files: exact, repeatable and stored with your aircraft record.
  3. 03
    Cut
    The CNC table cuts leather and fabric to the line, nesting pieces to save hide.
  4. 04
    Quilt & stitch
    Diamond, channel or custom quilting runs by machine, so every panel matches.
  5. 05
    Finish by hand
    Our hands assemble, close and fit every piece. The machine never finishes the job.
