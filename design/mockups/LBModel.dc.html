Squawk list: what wears out on a Bonanza.

After [number] Bonanzas through our shop, we know where they tire first, and how to make the fix outlast the original.

#SquawkOur fix
01
Sun-baked seat leather
Big side windows cook the seat tops.
Rebuild the foam; re-cover in UV-stable, burn-certified leather.
02
Cracked sidewalls and window trim
Original plastics go brittle with age and heat.
Repair and refinish, or 3D-print new trim; wrap panels to match.

The aft double doors and club seats take the most traffic.
Reinforce wear points; rebuild seat mechanisms.
