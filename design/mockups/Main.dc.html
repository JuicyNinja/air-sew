Your client portal

Most shops go dark until the job is done. We don't.

Air Sew is owner-operated: you work directly with the person who designs and signs off your cabin. From the day your airplane arrives, you watch every stage come together in your own portal.

  • Live job timeline — stage-by-stage progress for every zone, with your projected completion date.
  • Before, during and after — photos posted as each stage is signed off, plus a weekly digest.
  • Approvals without surprises — every finding or change comes with photos and a price, and a running total of approved additions stays in view.
  • Your records, for good — quote, work order, burn certs, records packet and 8130-3s where issued.
  • Invoices and payment — pay by card, ACH or wire.
  • One thread with the shop — portal, text and email in the same conversation. Reply from your phone; no login needed.
