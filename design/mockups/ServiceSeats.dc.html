What we do

From bare frame to your new favorite seat

  1. 01
    Strip and inspect
    Every seat comes down to the frame. We check structure, rails and recline mechanisms, and photograph anything that needs attention.
  2. 02
    Rebuild the comfort
    New foam shaped for support on long legs, layered for your height and how you sit.
  3. 03
    Pattern and cut
    Your patterns are digitized once, then CNC-cut so every panel matches, and a spare fits years from now.
  4. 04
    Sew and finish
    Stitched by hand-guided machines, with the details you chose: perforation, quilting, piping or embroidery.
  5. 05
    Fit and sign off
    Seats reinstalled, belts checked, logbook entries made and burn certs added to your records.
