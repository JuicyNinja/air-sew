From bare frame to your new favorite seat.

  1. 1
    Strip and inspect
    Every seat down to the frame; structure, rails and recline checked and photographed.
  2. 2
    Rebuild the comfort
    New foam shaped for support on long legs, layered for how you sit.
  3. 3
    Pattern and cut
    Patterns digitized once, then CNC-cut so every panel matches.
  4. 4
    Sew and finish
    Perforation, quilting, piping or embroidery, finished by hand.
  5. 5
    Fit and sign off
    Reinstalled, belts checked, logbook entries and burn certs added.
