Project spotlight

Citation CJ2 seat and floor restoration

Restoring the floors and seats in a 2003 Citation CJ2 jet based at Salt Lake City International. Every seat came off the airplane, was stripped to the frame, rebuilt with new foam, and re-covered in tan full-hide leather.

  • New carpet and floor panels throughout the cabin
  • New front crew seats, rebuilt mechanisms
  • New rear executive seats in full-hide leather
  • Removal and reinstallation coordinated at KSLC
