Heritage

Three generations of leatherwork from Bogotá, brought to the skies of northern Utah. Every stitch carries the legacy of the workshops where our family learned the craft.

Air Sew was built on the principle that an aircraft interior is more than seats and carpet. It's the space you spend your best hours in. We treat it that way, with premium materials, industrial design, and craftsmanship that takes its time.

Our story
What we do

Every surface in the cabin, done as one design.

All services
01

Seat Restoration

Frames inspected and repaired, new high-density foam carved to profile, then re-covered in leather, synthetic leather, or certified fabric.

02

Full Interior Restoration

Seats, side and door panels, headliner, carpet, and plastics, designed as one scheme and installed on the field.

03

Headliners

Sagging, stained, or brittle headliners replaced with new material that fits tight and stays put.

04

Floors & Carpet

Aviation-grade carpet, bound edges, and refurbished floor panels for a cabin that feels new underfoot.

05

Panels & Plastics

Side panels re-wrapped, interior plastics repaired and paint-matched to the new scheme.

06

Embroidery & Personalization

Logos, tail numbers, and custom artwork stitched in-house. Lighting, USB power, and modern touches on request.

The process

Fly in. Fly out. Everything between is handled.

01

Design appointment

Sit in your airplane with our designer. Choose materials, colors, stitching, and personalization. Leave with a fixed quote.

02

Fly in to Sky Park

Taxi to Hangar 27. We remove seats, panels, headliner, and carpet on the field and photograph everything.

03

Craft

Frames repaired, foam rebuilt, covers patterned and sewn, panels wrapped, plastics paint-matched. Progress photos along the way.

04

Install & fly out

Everything reinstalled, inspected, and documented for your logbook. Then you take a very different airplane home.
