Heritage & history

A family craft, three generations deep.

Nestled in the heart of Bogotá, Colombia, where leatherwork and upholstery are revered traditions, Air Sew's story begins with the skilled hands of our forebears.

Our story

In the workshops of our grandparents in Bogotá, the hum of sewing machines and the scent of leather became symbols of our family's dedication to their craft.

Three generations ago, our family embarked on a journey of craftsmanship, mastering the art of transforming leather and fabric into work of beauty and durability. Our parents expanded on that legacy, embracing innovation while honoring tradition. Today, at Air Sew, we bring that heritage to the skies of Utah.

Our commitment goes beyond crafting seats and interiors. It's about creating an experience that pays homage to our past while soaring toward the future. Each stitch we make, each design we conceive, carries the legacy of our forebears.

Timeline

From Bogotá to Sky Park.

Gen. 1

The Bogotá workshops

Our grandparents master leatherwork and upholstery, building a reputation on hard work, exceptional skill, and an unwavering commitment to quality.

Gen. 2

Tradition meets innovation

Our parents expand the family business, adopting new materials and techniques while preserving the old ways that make the work last.

Gen. 3

Air Sew takes flight

The craft moves to aviation. Air Sew opens at Sky Park Airport in Woods Cross, Utah, specializing in aircraft interiors for private owners, flight schools, and business aviation.

Today

Hangar 27

Fly-in / fly-out service from Sky Park, partnerships at South Valley and Salt Lake City International, and a growing list of Citations, Skylanes, Archers, and Kodiaks that leave looking brand new.
