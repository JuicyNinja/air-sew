Experimental aircraft interiors.

Built for builders

Your airplane is one of a kind. The interior should be too.

Experimental aircraft give us a freedom certified airplanes don't: no STC, no approved-parts list, and an owner who already knows every rivet. We use that freedom to build interiors that are lighter, more personal, and often more comfortable than anything in a production cabin.

Sport-style seats with bolsters and perforated inserts, carbon-look or leather-wrapped panels, custom embroidery, LED lighting, and carpet cut to your floor. Patterned from your actual frames and panels at Sky Park, or from parts you ship to us.

Still under construction? Start early.

The best time to design the interior is before the cabin closes up. Bring the project in or send photos and we will build covers to your frames and foam so everything drops in at final assembly.

Weight-conscious interiors where every ounce counts, without giving up comfort on long legs.
